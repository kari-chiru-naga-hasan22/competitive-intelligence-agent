# 60-Second Demo Story: Competitive Intelligence Agent

**Demo Time:** ~60 Seconds  
**Demo Question:** *"How has Acme Cloud's strategy changed over the last 90 days?"*  
**Core Thesis:** Pattern across time, not a single event.

---

## 1. The Hook & Problem (0:00 – 0:15)
> "In business, competitive moves don't happen in a single press release. They happen across months—scattered across pricing updates, enterprise security launches, messaging tweaks, and sales hiring.
>
> Today, if you ask a standard AI agent what a competitor is doing, it performs a point-in-time lookup. It sees only the latest event—like a recent price drop—and gives a shallow, misleading answer."

---

## 2. The Solution: Hindsight Persistent Memory (0:15 – 0:30)
> "Our Competitive Intelligence Agent introduces **persistent memory** powered by **Hindsight**.
>
> Instead of forgetting past signals, every competitor observation—whether a product launch or hiring surge—is preserved in long-term memory via Hindsight's `retain` API. When an executive asks a strategic question, the agent uses Hindsight's `recall` API to surface the entire 90-day trajectory and feeds that historical evidence into Gemini for synthesis."

---

## 3. The Live Query: Acme Cloud (0:30 – 0:45)
> "Let's ask our core question:
> **'How has Acme Cloud's strategy changed over the last 90 days?'**
>
> Instantly, the agent reconstructs 15 chronological events between July and September 2026:
> 1. **July:** Launched an AI analytics assistant, added enterprise SSO, and hired enterprise account executives.
> 2. **August:** Shifted all positioning to 'AI-first' and introduced automated executive reporting.
> 3. **September:** Bundled advanced AI into higher-tier enterprise packages, while dropping their Pro plan price from $49 down to $39."

---

## 4. The Intelligence Breakdown (0:45 – 1:00)
> "Notice how the agent breaks down the answer:
>
> - **Observed Changes (Facts):** Concrete, verified milestones directly grounded in memory—AI assistant rollout, enterprise security governance, and dual-tier pricing.
> - **Strategic Signal (Inference):** The agent identifies a **barbell go-to-market strategy**—lowering entry barriers at the low end to capture market share, while locking in enterprise accounts with premium security and bundled AI.
> - **Watch Next (Actionable):** Clear leading indicators to monitor, such as upcoming enterprise compliance certifications or usage-tier adjustments.
>
> Without memory, that $39 price drop looks like desperate discounting. **With persistent memory, it's revealed as a deliberate, two-pronged market offensive.**
>
> That is the power of persistent memory for competitive intelligence."
